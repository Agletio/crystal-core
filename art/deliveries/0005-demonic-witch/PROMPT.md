# Demonic witch — exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE production sprite sheet PNG for a dark gritty fantasy ARPG: the SAME DARK DEMONIC WITCH in two animation sequences, a WALK and CASTING A BUBBLE SHIELD AROUND HERSELF. Exactly TWELVE full-body poses in TWO ROWS of SIX evenly spaced cells. All frames in one image for character consistency. Transparent background. No labels or frame numbers.

STYLE: Diablo II-inspired bleak gothic dark fantasy, coarse grounded materials, worn soot-black cloth, tarnished metal, grey corpse-like skin and sinister restrained magic. Mature ominous demonic sorceress, NOT cute, anime, glossy, brightly cartoonish, or a pin-up. Pixel-art-ready with crisply grouped angular highlights and dark silhouettes, readable at 96px per cell after conversion; don't sacrifice convincing animation to tiny surface decoration.

LAYOUT: landscape 3:1 canvas, preferably 2304x768, containing a precise SIX-column TWO-row grid of invisible square 384x384 cells. Each cell has the same fixed camera, body scale and central pelvis location. Keep head and feet well inside every cell, and enough extra padding around the body for the final shield: witch height about 65% of cell, outer shield no more than 90% of cell. Never let limbs, cloth or magic overlap neighbouring cells. Row 1 six WALK frames, row 2 six SHIELD CAST frames. Whole body visible in every cell. Transparent alpha outside the art, no drawn checkerboard, ground, scenery, cast shadow, floor line, panels, border, text or watermark.

LOCKED CHARACTER MODEL ACROSS ALL TWELVE CELLS: one tall lean adult female witch, dark ash-grey skin, narrow angular face, stern shadowed eyes, exactly two short backward-curving black horns close to the temples, a close-fitting worn black hood that leaves both horns exposed, fixed small bone clasp at throat, faded burgundy fitted tunic under a long soot-black split-front coat, narrow rope belt, two tattered pointed coat tails reaching mid-calf, dark wrapped shins and sturdy dark ankle boots. No staff, book, bag, floating companions, weapons or extra accessories. Never change horns, face, hood, clasp, belt, hem length, body proportions or outfit between frames. Near leg and arm should remain distinguishable from the darker far leg and arm. Grim, practical and fully clothed.

Do not turn toward viewer or change camera angle. Upper-left overhead illumination on interior material planes, no bright silhouette rim on the witch. Restrained palette: warm charcoal, near-black brown, ash-grey, dull bone, dried-blood burgundy. Shield uses dark plum, muted violet, dusty lavender and tiny pale grey-violet highlights.

ROW 1 — WALK IN PLACE, one FULL two-step six-frame loop, evenly spaced phases. Pelvis does NOT drift side to side. Head nearly steady, subtle one-pixel-height weight shift. Boots trace convincing floor contact; coat tails drag with restrained secondary motion; no windstorm.
